# The Contractor

Run your farm as an agricultural contracting business in Farming Simulator 25. The Contractor replaces vanilla contracts with its own jobs, staff, farmers and business management.

## Intro video (YouTube)

[![Watch the video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/j-U_FkkbQbQ/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/j-U_FkkbQbQ)

## Getting started

1. Open the in-game menu and go to the new **Contractor** page.
2. On the **Overview** tab, create your company and give it a name.
3. Job offers arrive in the **Inbox**. Accept, decline or negotiate them, then on the **Jobs** tab choose who does each job and with which machines.
4. Hire workers from the **People** tab, or do the work yourself.
5. Place **parking slots** and **standing spots** or **seats** from the construction menu (Contractor tab), so machines coming back from jobs and idle staff have somewhere to go.
6. Keep seed, fertiliser, herbicide, lime and diesel in stock: jobs draw on your own farm's supplies.

The mod has its own pages in the in-game menu's **Help**, covering every part of it.

## Top features

- **A real contracting business**: vanilla contracts are switched off and work comes from the area's farmers instead, priced, negotiated, invoiced and paid late (or never)
- **Staff who actually do the work**: hired workers drive the vanilla AI helper to the field, work it and come home, with their own skills, growth, pay, morale, working hours, breaks, holidays, sickness and a live 3D portrait
- **Farmers with lives of their own**: the land you don't own belongs to named farmers with their own yards, machines and opinion of you, and they talk to each other
- **Running costs and risk**: insurance, overheads, compensation claims, legal cover and a cash forecast
- **Things happen**: theft, road accidents, fly-tipping, fallen trees, tipped trailers, spray drift, protests, and a bit of good news now and then
- **Machinery auctions**: a farmer's sale on a field, with a crowd, lots to view and sealed bids
- **Advanced Damage System support**: your staff run its workshop, customers bring machines in for repair, and farmers call you out to breakdowns
- **Full multiplayer**, with every farm running its own company

## Features

### Your company
- Found a contracting company for your farm from the new **Contractor** menu
- **Reputation** grows with good work and drops with failed, poor or botched jobs, and it decides how much work comes your way and how big. Six ranks, from Unknown to Prestigious Contractor
- **Advertising** campaigns, from the local newspaper through to national magazines and online, unlocking as your rank grows
- An **Important** board on the Overview for anything that needs your attention
- Your own **company logo** on the Overview and on placeable signs, freestanding or on a wall at three heights, which can also show designs of your own in single-player (see [Company logo and signs](#company-logo-and-signs))

### Jobs
- Vanilla contracts are switched off and replaced by the mod's own job offers, from the farmers whose land it is
- Every vanilla field job type: harvesting, mowing, tedding, baling, wrapping, cultivating, ploughing, sowing and planting, fertilising, spraying, weeding, hoeing, stone picking, plus spreading lime
- **Harvest & haul** (a harvester and a hauler, with the crop going to the customer's selling point) and **straw bale delivery** jobs
- Offers are matched to the machines you own, and some are booked ahead for crops that aren't ready yet
- **Price negotiation**: counter-offer, but push too hard and the customer walks away. A customer who thinks well of you gives more ground
- A **Jobs** tab of everything you've taken on, work that's ready now by due date and upcoming work by the day it can start. Choose who does each job (a worker or you) and with which machines, with a warning for anything wrong, and take a crew off again with Unallocate
- Due jobs are marked on the map, and the farmer leaves the field alone while you're on it
- Progress is read from the field itself, whoever's driving, with optional on-screen progress bars
- Every job gets a **quality score**. 100% is rare: it takes a crew with skill to spare, in good spirits, with well-kept machines, a clean run and a good day
- Missed deadlines cost reputation and goodwill

### Staff
- Hire workers from a shared pool of candidates, each with their own skills, potential, age and personality. Young ones come cheap and green; older ones know more but have less room to grow
- Hired workers drive the real vanilla AI helper to the field, do the work and come home. Stuck workers are reset and sent off again
- Skills for each kind of work and size of tractor grow with experience, and affect speed, quality, machine wear, and how clean the kit comes back
- **Working hours** (08:00 to 18:00), overtime, staying late, call-ins, and morning, lunch and afternoon **breaks**
- Monthly salaries at real UK rates, pay reviews, rising market value, and rival contractors trying to poach your best people before harvest
- **Morale**, bonuses (and the harvest bonus question every August), temperaments and rough patches, resignations and retirement
- Holiday requests through the Inbox, sickness, workplace injuries, and notice and redundancy pay when you let someone go
- Spraying certificates and paid training
- Each worker has their own name (in a choice of national styles), look and live 3D portrait, and can be renamed and restyled in the game's wardrobe
- An **office administrator** for invoicing and credit control, hired from the People tab
- Idle staff stand around the yard on placeable **standing spots**, or sit on placeable **seats** (a wooden chair, rocking chair or armchair), and go indoors when it rains. Stand by one and press the activate key to keep it for particular staff
- AI helper, AutoDrive, Courseplay and Follow Me vehicles get a driver from your staff, with no hourly helper wage

### Farmers
- The land you don't own is split between the area's **farmers**, listed with the local official on the People tab
- Each farmer has their own **relationship** with you, built by good work and worn down by poor work, failed jobs, ignored offers and chasing their debts. A farmer who falls out with you stops offering work, complains, and tells the neighbours
- **Farmers' yards** are worked out from the map's buildings, with the farmer standing about in theirs. The host can mark or rule out yards from the map, and dress them with standing spots and seats
- Every farmer has their own **machines**, the same ones every time, and you can change them to the models you like
- Yard jobs: **bales to a farmer's yard**, **selling a load** from their trailer, and an **emergency refuel** for a machine that's run dry on a field
- Fields change hands between farmers now and then, and with the setting on, farmers retire and hand on to a successor

### Running the business
- Jobs are **invoiced**. Customers pay late, and some never pay: write a debt off or pursue it
- Recurring **overheads**: employer's liability, plus optional public liability, machinery insurance (with levels of cover and a courtesy vehicle), a trade association, legal expenses insurance and a health and safety programme
- Compensation claims when a job goes wrong
- **Finances** tab with year-to-date figures and a cash forecast
- Job diesel, seed, fertiliser and other supplies come from your own farm stock, and a **Supplies** tab shows what you've got
- A **Logbook** of every job and every machine's history: who had it, what it was doing, and what state it came back in

### Equipment
- Machines out on a job leave the yard and come back to placeable **parking slots** (three sizes, for vehicles or implements, plus a harvester slot). Stand in one and press the activate key to keep it for some kinds of machine or particular machines. They're only visible in the construction menu, unless you turn on Show parking slots in the settings or press Right Ctrl+P
- Machines out on a job can't be sold or driven by anyone else until they're back

### Machinery auctions
- About once a year, sometimes twice, a farmer holds a sale on one of their fields, announced months ahead, with signs up around the area
- On the day the lots are laid out on the field and a crowd comes and goes
- Walk up to a lot to view it, leave a sealed bid, and drive away what you win. Anyone can bid, company or not

### Advanced Damage System support
- Careless or under-skilled workers cause breakdowns, and a worker won't take out a machine that's overdue for its service
- Your staff run ADS's workshop: field workers with mechanic, electrician, bodyworker or storekeeper trades work there whenever they're not out on a job (and can be trained up), with your office administrator as its advisor
- **Repair contracts**: customers bring their machines to placeable **repair zones**. Inspect, quote your price, then fix it, following the **Workshop** tab
- **Field breakdowns**: farmers' machines break down on their fields or in their yards, and you're called out
- Favours (fitting a part for a farmer) and **auction lot inspections** for farmers thinking of bidding
- Its oils, parts packs and spare parts show in the Supplies tab, and old parts go in a placeable **disposal skip**
- Only what you'd know about a machine's condition is ever shown

### Random events
- **Vehicle theft**, with police reports, insurance claims and a courtesy vehicle while you wait, and **diesel theft** overnight
- **Road accidents** on the way back from a job, and **spray drift complaints** after herbicide jobs: a skilled driver in well-kept machines is rarely to blame, but a poor one may land you with a claim
- **Botched jobs** and the claims that follow
- **Tipped trailers** on harvest and haul, with the spilt crop to clear up
- **Joyriders** tearing up grass fields, **fly-tipping** at field gates and on roadsides, and **fallen trees** across the roads after a storm, which turn into work for you
- **Protests** on the roads near your farm, slowing crews down
- **Good news**: a refund out of the blue, or a pleased farmer bringing tea and bacon butties
- Random events can be made rarer or switched off in the settings

### General
- Full multiplayer support, with everything farm by farm
- Settings for job pay rate, late invoices, random event frequency, staff breaks, farmer retirements, staff name style (British, Irish, American, French, German, Dutch, Scandinavian, Spanish, Italian, Polish or a mix) and more, in the game's own Settings page
- Works with FS25_EconomyScale, FS25_AdvancedDamageSystem, AutoDrive, Courseplay and Follow Me
- In-game help pages for every part of the mod
- Console commands for tailoring your game (see [Console commands](#console-commands))

## Company logo and signs

The Overview's company card and the signs (construction menu, Contractor tab: the freestanding **Company Sign** and the **Company Wall Sign**, 1.5 m or 3 m square, at 1 m, 3 m or 6 m) show a logo. To use your own, save it as `logo.dds` (or `logo.png`) in `Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/modSettings/FS25_TheContractor/` (the mod creates the folder, its `signs` folder and a `README.txt` explaining both when a save loads; the README is rewritten on every load, so edits to it are lost). To give one save a different logo, put it in a `savegame<N>` folder in there named after the save's slot (`savegame1/logo.dds` for save 1); a save without one uses the shared `logo.dds`.

- It must be square, with sides a multiple of 4 (512×512 or 1024×1024 work well). Any other shape is stretched to fit.
- DDS is best, ideally with mipmaps (GIANTS Texture Tool, or paint.net's DDS export). A PNG works too, but you'll get warnings in the game's log if you don't use DDS. If there's a DDS and a PNG of the same name, the DDS is used.
- A new logo shows the next time you open the menu, but replacing one you've already seen needs a game restart.
- The logo is read from your own PC only: in multiplayer, your logo shows only on your own farm's signs, and other players see their own logo file, or the default.

In single-player, a sign can show a design of your own instead. Put square `.dds` (or `.png`) images in the `signs` folder; each becomes a design you pick when placing a sign, named after its file (`for_sale.dds` is "For Sale"). New images show after you reload the save, and the same size and format rules apply. In multiplayer there's no design picker and every sign shows the company logo.

A wall sign's height is that of its bottom edge. Place it from just in front of the wall, with its face pointing away from it; the construction menu's free mode helps get it flush against a building.

## Console commands

Type `tcHelp` in the developer console to list the mod's commands. Each group (`tcWorker`, `tcJob`, `tcFinance`, `tcCompany`, `tcEvent`, `tcFarmer`, `tcYard`, `tcAuction`) lists its own subcommands when typed on its own. They're all documented in [docs/commands.md](docs/commands.md).
